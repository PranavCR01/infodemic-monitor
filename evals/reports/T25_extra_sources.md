## Left for a person

- **v159/c1 (vibration plate = an hour of jogging).** Retrieved: whole-body-vibration squats at 3.8-7.3 METs (PMID:39820024), plus the labelling's Rittweger and Kang VO2 figures. A standard jogging MET value (e.g. the Compendium of Physical Activities) would make this CONTRADICTED by arithmetic, but no allowed source returns one. Adding a compendium-type fact-of-record source would decide this claim and v159 would become MISINFO.
- **v85/c1 (wind turbine syndrome).** The 2026 PNAS longitudinal study (PMID:42154547) finds no detectable health effects and excludes moderate-to-large effects. I kept INSUFFICIENT because it cannot exclude a syndrome in a minority and does not speak to 'recognised as a disease'. A person may reasonably call it CONTRADICTED; if so, v85 becomes MISINFO.
- **v120/c3 (seawater has 118 elements).** False on basic chemistry (synthetic elements), but nothing on the allow-list states it. Another fact-of-record gap rather than a research gap.
- **The 'unproven claim' decision.** On this sample, more databases do not resolve 'source says false, evidence is silent': 0 of 60 claims and 0 of 39 videos change. The gap is about what the label means, not about missing data. That supports deciding on an explicit unproven/unsupported-claim verdict or label rather than expecting retrieval to close it.
