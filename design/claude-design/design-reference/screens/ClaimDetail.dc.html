What the evidence shows

CONTRADICTED2 sources contradict · 0 support

A randomised trial found a hot drink eased cold and flu symptoms but did not test or show a cure. WHO guidance describes no cure for seasonal flu; care focuses on rest, fluids and, for people at higher risk, antiviral medicine.

Limitations. The trial was small and measured symptoms shortly after drinking; it did not measure how long illness lasted.
