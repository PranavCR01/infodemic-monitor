★ 1. Drinking hot water every morning cures the flu.

CONTRADICTED 2 sources contradict, 0 support.

A randomised trial found a hot drink eased cold and flu symptoms but did not test or show a cure. WHO guidance describes no cure for seasonal flu.

Limitations: the trial was small and measured symptoms shortly after drinking, not illness duration.
